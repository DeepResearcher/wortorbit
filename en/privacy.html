WortOrbit – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026 · applies to WortOrbit 1.0

1. Who is responsible

Narli-Digital
info@narli-digital.de

2. In short

  • WortOrbit has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app does not connect to the internet and sends no data to us or to anyone else.
  • Your learning progress, your name and your avatar stay on your device.
  • Your spoken answers are converted into text by your device's speech recognition service. Its provider (for example Google) processes the audio under its own terms.

3. What the app processes, and why

3.1 Spoken answers (microphone)

The microphone is only active while the app waits for your answer during a round. WortOrbit passes the audio to your device's speech recognition service (Android's SpeechRecognizer) and receives the recognized text. If your device supports it, recognition runs on the device. Otherwise the speech recognition provider on your device (for example Google) processes the audio under its own privacy terms. We have no control over that provider and do not receive the audio.

WortOrbit does not record or store any audio. It only keeps the recognized text of your answer, on your device (see 3.2).

Legal basis: providing the service you requested (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

3.2 Learning data on your device

Stored only on your device, separately for each language direction:

  • which question you answered and when
  • the recognized answer text
  • the result (correct, wrong, passed) and the answer time
  • the mastery score of each word, your rounds and statistics
  • your settings, the name you enter, and your avatar image

This data is not transmitted to us or to anyone else. It is not included in device backups, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

3.3 Avatar photo

You can choose a photo for your avatar with Android's photo picker; the app only gets access to the photo you pick. The avatar is created entirely on your device: the photo is turned upright and reduced, and Android's built-in face detector finds the face on the device so the picture can be centred. The photo is not sent anywhere and not stored; only the finished avatar image stays on your device. You can change or remove your avatar in your profile at any time.

3.4 Reading aloud

Questions and pronunciations are spoken by your device's text-to-speech engine. You manage its language packs in the device settings.

3.5 Permissions

WortOrbit asks for the microphone permission for spoken answers. It does not use the camera and does not ask for camera, location, contacts or storage permissions. The app declares internet access for an optional online feature that may come in a later version; version 1.0 makes no network connections. Before any such feature is offered, this policy will be updated and you will be asked for your consent in the app.

4. Recipients

We do not pass data to anyone, and we do not sell or share data for advertising. Your device's speech recognition service is a component of your device and is not engaged by us.

5. How long data is kept

Learning data, your name and your avatar stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. We do not keep any data about you.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent with effect for the future. Since your learning data never leaves your device, you control it directly; you can delete it by uninstalling the app. For questions, contact us at info@narli-digital.de.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

7. Children

WortOrbit is intended for learners aged 13 and over. The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

8. Changes

We will update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.

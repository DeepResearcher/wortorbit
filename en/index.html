WortOrbit

Hear the clue, say the word: a spoken vocabulary game in 4 languages.

WortOrbit turns learning vocabulary into a letter game. For every letter you hear a clue in your own language and say the answer out loud in the language you are learning.

  • All 12 directions between Turkish, German, English and Arabic
  • Six levels from A1 to C2, thousands of words and pictures that show their meaning
  • No account, no ads, no tracking: your progress stays on your device
